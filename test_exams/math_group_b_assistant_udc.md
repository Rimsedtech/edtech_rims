## Q38
If $P_3^n = 504$, find $n$.

- [ ] 10
- [ ] 6
- [ ] 12
- [x] 9

**Difficulty:** hard

$9 \times 8 \times 7 = 504$. Therefore, $n=9$.

## Q39
Marcus pays Rs. 1,000 to Toby with Rs. 50, Rs. 20 and Rs. 10 notes. If the total number of notes given is 40 and the number of Rs. 20 notes is 8, find the number of Rs. 50 notes in the payment.

- [ ] 10
- [ ] 18
- [ ] 9
- [x] 13

**Points:** 2
**Difficulty:** hard
**Explanation:** 
Let $x$ be 50s and $y$ be 10s.
$x + y + 8 = 40 \Rightarrow x + y = 32$.
$50x + 10y + 160 = 1000 \Rightarrow 50x + 10y = 840 \Rightarrow 5x + y = 84$.
Subtracting: $4x = 52 \Rightarrow x = 13$.

## Q43
A sector of 56°, cut out from a circle, contains 17.6 $cm^2$. Find the radius of the circle.

- [x] 6 cm
- [ ] 6.5 cm
- [ ] 5 cm
- [ ] 5.6 cm

**Points:** 2
**Difficulty:** hard
**Explanation:** 
Area $= (\theta/360) \times \pi r^2$. 
$17.6 = (56/360) \times (22/7) \times r^2 \Rightarrow r^2 = 36 \Rightarrow r = 6$.

## Q47
By selling an article, I lose $1/3$ of selling price. The loss percentage is:

- [ ] 15%
- [ ] 20%
- [ ] 30%
- [x] 25%

**Points:** 1
**Difficulty:** easy
**Explanation:** 
$L = 1/3 SP \Rightarrow CP = SP + 1/3 SP = 4/3 SP$. 
Loss% $= (1/3 SP / 4/3 SP) \times 100 = 25\%$.

## Q48
