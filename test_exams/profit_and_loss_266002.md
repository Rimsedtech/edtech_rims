## Answer Key

### Q1
Step 1: CP = ₹400,  SP = ₹500
Step 2: Profit = SP − CP = 500 − 400 = ₹100
Step 3: Profit % = (Profit / CP) × 100 = (100 / 400) × 100 = 25%
Correct Answer: (b) 25%

### Q2
Step 1: CP = ₹800, P% = 20%
Step 2: SP = (100 + P%) / 100 × CP = (120 / 100) × 800 = ₹960
Correct Answer: (c) ₹960

### Q3
Step 1: SP = ₹440, P% = 10%
Step 2: CP = SP × 100 / (100 + P%) = 440 × 100 / 110 = ₹400
Correct Answer: (c) ₹400

### Q4
Step 1: CP = ₹1,200, SP = ₹900
Step 2: Loss = CP − SP = 1200 − 900 = ₹300
Step 3: Loss % = (Loss / CP) × 100 = (300 / 1200) × 100 = 25%
Correct Answer: (c) 25%

### Q5
Step 1: CP = ₹750, L% = 20%
Step 2: SP = (100 − L%) / 100 × CP = (80 / 100) × 750 = ₹600
Correct Answer: (b) ₹600

### Q6
Step 1: A's CP = ₹1,000
Step 2: A → B at 10% profit: SP(A) = (110/100) × 1000 = ₹1,100
Step 3: B → C at 5% profit: SP(B) = (105/100) × 1100 = ₹1,155
Step 4: C → D at 20% profit: SP(C) = (120/100) × 1155 = ₹1,386
Correct Answer: (c) ₹1,386

### Q7
Step 1: Let CP = x
Step 2: SP at 20% profit = (120/100)x; SP at 30% profit = (130/100)x
Step 3: Difference: (130x − 120x) / 100 = 200
Step 4: 10x / 100 = 200 → x = ₹2,000
Correct Answer: (c) ₹2,000

### Q8
Step 1: Rule — When the same SP is used with equal gain% and loss%, there is ALWAYS a net LOSS.
Step 2: Net Loss % = (Common %)² / 100 = (15)² / 100 = 225 / 100 = 2.25%
Step 3: Verification — Let SP = 120 for both.
Step 4: Watch 1 (15% gain): CP₁ = 120 × 100/115 = ₹104.35
Step 5: Watch 2 (15% loss): CP₂ = 120 × 100/85 = ₹141.18
Step 6: Total CP = ₹245.53, Total SP = ₹240 → Loss ≈ 2.25%
Correct Answer: (b) 2.25% loss

### Q9
Step 1: CP per orange = ₹10 / 20 = ₹0.50
Step 2: SP per orange = ₹12 / 10 = ₹1.20
Step 3: Profit per orange = 1.20 − 0.50 = ₹0.70
Step 4: Profit% = (0.70 / 0.50) × 100 = 140%
Correct Answer: (c) 140%

### Q10
Step 1: Price decreased by 25% → Consumer saved = (25/100) × 180 = ₹45
Step 2: This ₹45 now buys 3 kg extra at the new (reduced) price.
Step 3: New (reduced) price = ₹45 / 3 = ₹15 per kg
Step 4: Original price = New price × 100 / (100 − 25) = 15 × 100/75 = ₹20 per kg
Correct Answer: (c) ₹20/kg

### Q11
Step 1: Let CP = x → P% = x% (given: profit% = CP numerically)
Step 2: x = 96 × 100 / (100 + x)
Step 3: x(100 + x) = 9600
Step 4: x² + 100x − 9600 = 0
Step 5: x = [−100 ± √(10000 + 38400)] / 2 = [−100 ± √48400] / 2
Step 6: √48400 = 220
Step 7: x = (−100 + 220) / 2 = 120/2 = 60 (negative root rejected)
Step 8: Verification: SP = (100+60)/100 × 60 = 160/100 × 60 = ₹96 ✓
Correct Answer: (d) ₹60

### Q12
Step 1: Let original CP = x
Step 2: Case 1 (Original): SP₁ = (110/100) × x
Step 3: Case 2 (New): New CP = (80/100)x; SP₂ = (150/100) × (80/100)x = (120/100)x
Step 4: SP₂ − SP₁ = 10 → 120x/100 − 110x/100 = 10
Step 5: 10x/100 = 10 → x = ₹100
Correct Answer: (b) ₹100

### Q13
